About Project

The Environmental Action Network (EAN) is a web application platform for users to report environmental issues and collaborate on solutions. It can be regarded as a social media platform dedicated to environmental concerns.

  • Community-driven crowdsourcing for environmental issues
  • Social media for environmentalists

Why is EAN Needed?

  • Spreading awareness and encouraging action
  • Building connections between like-minded individuals
  • Quick delivery of effective solutions
  • Creating a knowledge hub for environmentalists

Theme Alignment

Theme: Tech for Social Good

EAN aligns with the hackathon theme by leveraging technology to make the world a better place and fostering a greener, more sustainable planet.

How EAN Works

EAN uses a five-step process:

  1. Reporting and displaying issues through user-submitted reports.
  2. Map visualization for better analysis of environmental reports.
  3. Community forums for discussion and collaborative solutions.
  4. Advanced analytics for deeper insights (if time permits).
  5. AI consultation for enhanced problem-solving (optional).

Additional Features (Optional)

  • Volunteer management system
  • Advanced analytics with geographical and environmental insights
  • AI chatbot integration for consultations
